Privacy policy

Pets Vision · Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version: Pets Vision has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your photos and videos stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what's needed to handle the optional Pro purchase.

Camera and microphone

Pets Vision uses the camera to show the animal filters live. The microphone is used only while you record a video, and only if you allow it. Everything is processed on your phone. The app never uploads or stores your camera images or sound anywhere else.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos are saved to your phone's gallery only when you tap Save. When you tap Share, they go only to the app or person you choose, through your phone's share menu. Pets Vision only adds to your gallery; it does not read your existing photos.

Settings

Your choices in the app (for example, whether videos record sound) are stored only on your phone.

Purchases

Pets Vision Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Pets Vision never sees your payment details.

To check whether you own Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history for Pets Vision, and basic technical information such as your device type, system version, app version, country and IP address. This information is not linked to your name or email, and is not used for advertising or tracking. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Pets Vision is suitable for all ages. It does not ask for personal information and does not show ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
